# Backend Lead — Northwind Payments (Helsinki / Hybrid)

Northwind is scaling its payments platform after a recent funding round. We're
looking for a Backend Lead to own the core transaction services and grow the team.

## What you'll do
- Own architecture and delivery of our high-throughput payments services.
- Lead and line-manage a team of 6+ backend engineers.
- Set technical direction and mentor across the group.

## Requirements
- 5+ years of backend engineering experience.
- Strong in Python and/or Go.
- Hands-on experience with event-driven / streaming architectures at scale.
- Deep PostgreSQL knowledge.
- Proven people management — you've line-managed a team of at least 6 engineers.
- Production Kubernetes experience.
- Experience in fintech or payments is a strong plus.
- Fluent English.

## Compensation
Competitive salary based on experience.
